Welcome to Comma Clips

Let's connect to your comma. It needs to be switched on, on the same Wi-Fi as this computer, with SSH turned on (in the comma's settings, turn on SSH and enter your GitHub username so it accepts your key; it's under Developer or Network depending on the software version).
